title=Infinite Capacity / 无限容量
description=[h1]Infinite Capacity[/h1]\nRemoves the character pickup hard cap and container capacity limits with detailed sandbox configuration. Drag-and-drop, Loot All, and “Put in Container...” remain available.\n\n[h2]Configurable features for Build 42.20[/h2]\n• Adjustable from the built-in Settings → Mods page and synchronized with sandbox settings\n• Character inventory: Vanilla / Custom limit (1–10000) / Unlimited\n• Character capacity is reflected in the inventory display; Unlimited shows 10000\n• Containers: Vanilla / Original-capacity multiplier (1–100×) / Unlimited\n• Separate toggles for bags, world containers, and vehicle containers\n• Optional vehicle cargo mass exclusion, enabled by default\n• Multiplayer settings are controlled by the server's sandbox configuration; regular players have read-only controls\n• Defaults remain unlimited to preserve the behavior of previous releases\n\n[h2]Compatibility and safety[/h2]\n• Updated against Project Zomboid Build 42.20.3 stable\n• Build 41 retains the original all-unlimited compatibility mode\n• Multiplayer requires the mod on both server and clients\n• Preserves item-type rules, per-item bag size, in-vehicle heavy-item restrictions, trading, safehouse, and transaction checks\n• Floor-item safety and ItemNumbersLimitPerContainer remain vanilla\n\n[h2]Credits[/h2]\nThe concept combines Remove Loot Restriction (Workshop 2885648642) and Bypass Container Capacity Limit (Workshop 3396168708).\n\n[hr][/hr]\n\n[h1]无限容量[/h1]\n解除角色拾取硬上限和容器容量限制，并可在沙盒设置中精细控制。拖放、全部拾取和右键“放入容器……”均可正常使用。\n\n[h2]Build 42.20 可配置功能[/h2]\n• 可在系统自带的“设置 → 模组”页面直接调整，并与沙盒设置同步\n• 角色物品栏：原版 / 自定义容量上限（1–10000）/ 无限\n• 角色容量会真实反映在物品栏右上角；无限模式显示 10000\n• 容器容量：原版 / 原容量倍率（1–100 倍）/ 无限\n• 可分别控制背包与手提包、世界容器、车辆容器\n• 可让车辆容器中的货物不计入整车物理质量（默认开启）\n• 多人游戏由服务器沙盒设置统一控制；普通玩家只读\n• 默认仍为角色和容器无限，更新后保持旧版行为\n\n[h2]兼容性与安全[/h2]\n• 已按 Project Zomboid Build 42.20.3 正式版更新\n• Build 41 保留原有的全部无限兼容模式\n• 多人游戏需要服务器和客户端同时安装并启用\n• 保留物品类型、背包单件尺寸、车内重物、交易、保险屋及多人交易一致性检查\n• 地面物品安全限制及 ItemNumbersLimitPerContainer 服务器上限保持原版\n\n[h2]鸣谢[/h2]\n功能构想结合了 Remove Loot Restriction（工坊 2885648642）和 Bypass Container Capacity Limit（工坊 3396168708）。\n\n[h2]IDs / 编号[/h2]\nWorkshop ID: 3546219719\nMod ID: RemoveCapacityAndPickUpLimits
